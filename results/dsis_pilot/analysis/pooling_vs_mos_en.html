Temporal pooling rules vs MOS (DSIS pilot)

7 participants, 84 videos, 8 scenes; no participant was rejected. Within each scene, all of its videos (every shot together) are ranked by MOS and by the rule's score, and the Spearman correlation (SRCC) between the two rankings is computed. The 8 scene SRCCs are averaged in Fisher z space: equal-weighted gives every scene the same weight; weighted uses n − 3 (number of videos minus 3), so scenes with more videos count more.

1. Scene-averaged SRCC per rule

Sorted by the weighted average. Small scenes (heros3 and ratchet, 5 videos each) inflate the equal-weighted average, so the weighted average is the one to read. Click a row to show that rule in the scatter plots below.
