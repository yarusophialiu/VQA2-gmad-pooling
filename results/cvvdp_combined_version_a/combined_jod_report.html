CVVDP scores for the DSIS experiment

Each of the 239 gMAD-selected videos in Version A was scored with ColorVideoVDP as one 4.5 s clip (chunk 1 + chunk 2 + chunk 3), as participants see it, against its shot's 1080p120 reference. Each JOD is then mapped to the five-level DSIS impairment scale.

DSIS levels

CVVDP has no official DSIS mapping. Bins are 1 JOD wide, counted down from the reference (JOD 10); 1 JOD is the difference that 75% of viewers would notice when choosing the better of two clips, so within 0.5 JOD of the reference is treated as imperceptible.

Method

  • ColorVideoVDP v0.5.6 (git e837f06), video mode, display model standard_fhd, torch 2.6.0+cu124. It reproduces the earlier per-chunk scores exactly.
  • Test and reference are resized to 1920×1080 (bilinear) and resampled to 120 Hz by frame repetition, giving 540 frames per clip; temporal padding replicate.
  • The three 1.5 s chunks are joined in memory, so temporal filtering runs across chunk boundaries. 1080p120 test chunks use the reference file, as the experiment does.
  • Per-frame JOD proxies (540 per video) are in frames/; the scores are in combined_jod_dsis.csv. Built by gmad/score_combined_cvvdp.py and gmad/build_combined_jod_report.py.
