Multi-scene gMAD selection: two experiment versions

gMAD was run on 28 shots, one content group per shot, using a single local CVVDP scoring setup (standard_fhd, 120 Hz) and the same parameters as the bistro run. Two experiment-sized versions were then drawn from all gMAD pairs: Version A covers every scene, Version B covers a subset of scenes.

Method

gMAD

Each shot is its own group, giving 9³ = 729 three-chunk videos (1.5 s per chunk). The 15 temporal pooling rules take turns as defender and attacker; within each of 3 quality bins, gMAD finds the pair the defender rates as nearly equal and the attacker rates as most different. Negative per-frame scores are skipped and time constants are converted to 120 Hz.

Perceptibility label

For each pair, take the largest |ΔJOD| across the 15 pooling models:

  • Likely imperceptible max gap < 0.5 JOD, close to chance
  • Possibly imperceptible 0.5–1 JOD
  • Perceptible ≥ 1 JOD (about 75% of observers pick the better video)

A pair is flagged as imperceptible only when every model predicts a small difference, so this is a conservative model-based screen, not a human threshold.

Two versions

  • Version A: 10 pairs per scene, taking shots in turn. Pairs that cover a new (defender, attacker, quality bin) cell come first, then larger normalized attack gaps. Perceptibility is labelled, not used to exclude pairs.
  • Version B: the top 5 scenes by median max gap, 25 pairs each, excluding likely-imperceptible pairs.

Scene ranking

Median gap is the median, over all gMAD pairs of a scene, of each pair's largest model gap. Version B takes the top 5. Rows with a blue left edge are the scenes in Version B.

The top two Version B scenes may be overrated

The input contains 24 negative per-frame scores, from the 30/60 fps chunks of gallery/gallery_01 (12 frames), rogue/rogue_01 (6 frames), sibenik/sibenik_01 (6 frames). These frames show sharp quality drops and were skipped, as in the bistro run. Worst-window and percentile pooling react strongly to brief drops, so part of the high ranking of sibenik and gallery may come from a few outlier frames. For a more robust Version B, pass the scenes explicitly, for example --scenes forza spiderman heros3 ratchet lego.
