DSIS training videos

The 5 practice videos played by run_dsis_training.m, in presentation order. Each is compared with its shot's 1080p120 reference, as in the experiment. The CVVDP level is the video's 4.5 s CVVDP JOD mapped to the DSIS scale. Participants are shown a range from one level below it up to it (level 1 stays 1), after rating and top-left during the test video when SHOW_EXPECTED_LEVEL is on.
